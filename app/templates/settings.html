Troubleshooting
Connection expired or still says Not Connected

Find LMS+ in Claude Connectors or ChatGPT Plugins. Choose Connect or Reconnect, authorize in LMS+, then refresh Settings.

Can’t add the connector

Claude. If your Free plan’s custom connector slot is full, remove a connector you no longer use before adding LMS+.

ChatGPT. Open ChatGPT on the web with a supported paid plan. For school or work accounts, ask your workspace admin to enable custom plugins.

Connected, but the chat can’t find assignments

Claude. In the chat, open +, choose Connectors, and turn on LMS+. If prompted, select Allow once.

ChatGPT. Type @LMS+ and select the plugin before asking about your assignments.

Your Questions Answered.

Everything you need to know about preparing for and using LMS+.

Why build a modern Bahria student portal?

We wanted a beautiful, smooth, and fast alternative to the outdated official interface.

Built by students, for students, LMS+ (LMS Plus) is the ultimate Bahria LMS and Bahria CMS portal reimagined for 2026. Enjoy pure speed, a built-in GPA calculator, and no more constant CMS logouts.

Is this a replacement for Bahria LMS?

LMS+ is a third-party application built to enhance your experience.

It acts as a powerful alternative interface for the official Bahria LMS and Bahria CMS, providing a faster and more intuitive way to manage your assignments and lectures.

How does it sync with Bahria CMS?

Direct, secure synchronization whenever you log in.

LMS+ securely communicates with Bahria CMS to instantly fetch your attendance, timetable, and provisional results. Your credentials are never stored in plain text and are only used for secure authentication.

Are my credentials safe?

Yes, 100% encrypted and protected.

We use industry-standard encryption for all data transfers. Your credentials are encrypted and only used to interact with the official portal on your behalf.
